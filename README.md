### 2. Website Conversion Funnel Analysis | Python & SQL
> *Understanding where the customers are leaving the funnel*

**The Problem:** A business had user activity spread across multiple tables but lacked a clear understanding of how users moved through the purchase journey, where they abandoned it, and which user segments had the lowest conversion rates.

**What I Did:**
- Analysed **90,400 users across 5 relational tables** using Python and SQL.
- Built a user-level funnel model and reusable SQL views to track user progression and identify drop-off stages.
- Conducted **Funnel Analysis** that revealed 92.50% abandonment at Payment → Confirmation and the largest user loss of 39,170 at Search → Payment.
- Compared funnel performance across **device types, gender, and monthly user groups** uncovering conversion gap between Mobile (1.00%) and Desktop (0.25%).
- Validated analytical results against source data and translated findings into targeted recommendations for checkout and desktop journey optimization.

**Impact:** Revealed that the biggest conversion opportunity lies in improving the existing purchase journey rather than simply attracting more visitors. Provided a data-backed basis for prioritising critical funnel improvements and reducing potential revenue leakage.

### 3. Retail Growth & Retention Analysis | Python & Power BI
> *Diagnosing a silent customer churn crisis with data*

**The Problem:** A retail business had seemingly stable revenue but was bleeding customers without realising it.

**What I Did:**
- Identified **stable monthly revenue (₹170K-180K)** masking a deeper retention problem
- Uncovered **91% customer inactivity rate** and **87% disengagement among high-value customers**
- Analysed sales trends, customer behaviour, and cohort retention end-to-end
- Built an **interactive 3-page Power BI dashboard** to visualise the findings for stakeholders
- Delivered strategic recommendations to reverse churn and re-engage dormant customers

**Impact:** Gave the business a clear picture of *where* value was being lost and *how* to win it back.
